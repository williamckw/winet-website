Website Maintenance Checklist for Growing Businesses

A repeatable checklist for updates, backups, mobile tests, security and enquiry tracking.

Understand the goal

Start by identifying the business objective. A website or a business system should make a task easier, communicate services clearly or help customers take the next step.

Plan around users

Document the users, key pages or workflows, inputs, decision makers and expected outcomes. Confirm what needs to connect with existing systems and who will maintain the result.

Implement with purpose

Plan clear navigation, readable content, mobile-friendly layouts and accessible forms. Use descriptive page titles, relevant headings, internal links and authentic imagery where available.

Support continuous improvement

Evaluate ongoing costs, content updates, performance, backups and enquiry tracking. Improving after launch is as important as publishing the first version.
